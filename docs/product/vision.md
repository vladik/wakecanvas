# Product vision

WakeCanvas is an offline platform for participatory installations where artists
create the experience and communities bring it to life.

An installation can turn a wall, floor, table, screen, projection, or room into a
shared canvas. Participation may come from movement, touch, sound, messages,
photographs, artwork, phones, or physical sensors.

## People

- **Creators** build Experiences with the public SDK.
- **Operators** install, configure, activate, and diagnose Experiences.
- **Participants** bring the active Experience instance to life.

## Product principles

1. A venue must continue operating without internet access.
2. One installation is one understandable application and service.
3. Experiences build and ship independently from the platform.
4. A failed Experience must not make platform management unavailable.
5. Installation and operation must be possible without a developer present.
6. The system starts simple and adds hardware or native processes only when an
   Experience requires them.

The initial reference Experience is **Living Gallery**, a workshop installation
that combines an ambient photographic collage with prominently featured participant
artwork captured from a local intake station.
